Timestamp Converter

Convert between Unix timestamps and human-readable dates. All conversions run in your browser.

About Unix Timestamps

A Unix timestamp represents the number of seconds that have elapsed since January 1, 1970, 00:00:00 UTC (the Unix epoch). It is a standard way to represent points in time in programming, databases, and APIs.

This tool auto-detects whether your input is in seconds (10 digits) or milliseconds (13 digits). It displays the result in UTC, your local timezone, and ISO 8601 format.
